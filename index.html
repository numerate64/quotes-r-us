What belongs here

Funny.
Thoughtful.
Shareable.

A small set of rules keeps a very large collection useful.

01

Keep it quotable

Short enough to remember, clear enough to stand on its own, and suitable for a general audience.

02

Credit the voice

Name the author when known. Use Anonymous only when the source genuinely cannot be identified.

03

Tag with care

A few accurate tags make discovery better than a pile of almost-related ones.
